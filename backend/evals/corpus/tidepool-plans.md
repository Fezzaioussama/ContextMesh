# Tidepool plans and pricing

Tidepool is the field-research workspace sold by Halvard Systems. It comes in three plans;
prices are per user per month.

## Driftwood (free)

- Up to 3 projects and 2 GB of storage per workspace.
- Version history is kept for 7 days.
- Support: community forum only.

## Harbor

- $14 per user per month billed annually, or $17 billed monthly.
- 50 GB of storage per user.
- Version history is kept for 90 days.
- Nonprofit organizations get 40% off Harbor.

## Lighthouse

- $29 per user per month, billed annually only, with a minimum of 25 seats.
- Single sign-on through SAML, and audit logs retained for 7 years.
- Unlimited version history.

## Refunds

Annual Harbor and Lighthouse subscriptions can be refunded within 21 days of purchase.
Monthly plans are not refunded.
