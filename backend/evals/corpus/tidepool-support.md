# Tidepool support policy

## Channels

- Driftwood: the community forum.
- Harbor: email support, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Lisbon time.
- Lighthouse: email support plus 24/7 phone support.

## First-response targets

| Severity | Harbor | Lighthouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 (service down) | 1 business day | 30 minutes |
| P2 (feature broken) | 2 business days | 4 hours |
| P3 (question) | 3 business days | 1 business day |

## Escalation

A Lighthouse customer can escalate any open P1 or P2 ticket by asking for "Code Amber",
which pages the on-call support lead.

## Offices

The support team works from Lisbon and Montreal.
